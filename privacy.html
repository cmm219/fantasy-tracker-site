Privacy policy

Fantasy Tracker uses Google access only to read and update the specific Google Sheets the owner picked when signing in (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files.

No Google data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's private server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
